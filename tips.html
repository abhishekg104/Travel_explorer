Travel Tips

Simple things worth checking before any trip.

Documents

Check passport validity and visa rules well before you book.

Budget

Set a daily budget and keep some cash plus one backup card.

Packing

Look at the forecast on the destination page and pack for it.

Safety

Share your plan with someone and keep copies of key documents.

Local transport

Look up metro or bus options in advance, they are often cheaper than taxis.

Language

Learn a few basic phrases and download an offline map.
